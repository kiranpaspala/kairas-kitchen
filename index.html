👶 Baby food safety

This app gives general meal ideas, not medical advice. Prepare food in a texture your baby can safely manage. Cook egg, meat and fish thoroughly and remove bones/hard pieces. Avoid honey before 12 months and avoid adding salt. Follow your health professional's advice for allergens and individual feeding needs.
